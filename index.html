Small Language Models in Agentic RAG

Empirical study of where small models fail in agentic retrieval over financial documents. Four SLMs (1.7B to 8B) plus a 117B ceiling on FinanceBench, naive RAG against an agentic ReAct loop, hybrid BM25 and BGE dense retrieval with reranking, a 10-category failure taxonomy, and dual 70B judges agreeing at Cohen's kappa 0.65. Full harness on Modal serverless GPUs.

Python · Modal · BM25 + BGE · ReAct · FinanceBench
