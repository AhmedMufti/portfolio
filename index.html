Research: where small language models fail.

Evaluating the Efficacy and Failure Modes of Small Language Models in Agentic RAG Workflows for Financial Document Reasoning

Sole author. Independent research, FAST-NUCES, 2026. Manuscript in Springer LNCS format; arXiv preprint in preparation.

Analysts are being offered small, local language models as a private alternative to frontier APIs, but the field reports headline accuracy without much insight into what goes wrong underneath. I evaluated four contemporary small models (Qwen3-1.7B, SmolLM3-3B, Phi-4-mini, Llama-3.1-8B) and a 117B open-weight ceiling on FinanceBench, under plain retrieval-augmented generation and an agentic ReAct loop.

I built the whole harness on Modal serverless GPUs: hybrid BM25 and BGE retrieval with a cross-encoder reranker, the model-serving classes, the agent loop, and a two-judge adjudication pipeline using 70B-class models from two different vendors. Every accuracy is reported with a Wilson 95% confidence interval. Two findings stood out: the agentic loop lowered accuracy for every model, and the dominant failure was numerical error, not retrieval, even when the right numbers were in the context.
